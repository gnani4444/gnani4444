## Featured Projects

### [Truth Verifier](https://www.truthverifier.in/) · AI Health Misinformation Detector

**The problem:** Health misinformation spreads fast on social media. A single viral reel claiming "this herb cures diabetes" or "vaccines cause autism" reaches millions before anyone checks whether it's true. Most people have no quick way to verify health claims they encounter online — they either trust them blindly or spend 30 minutes Googling.

**How it works:** Paste an Instagram or YouTube link → the system downloads the video, transcribes the audio with Gradium STT, extracts individual health claims using a TinyFish Agent, and cross-references each claim against medical sources in real time. Results stream back progressively via SSE — you see verdicts appear as each claim is resolved, not after everything finishes.

**How it helps:** Every claim gets a clear verdict — `TRUE`, `FALSE`, or `UNVERIFIABLE` — with cited sources. No opinion, no guesswork. A 10-minute reel gets fact-checked in seconds. The vision mode also catches on-screen text (overlays, captions, infographics) that audio-only transcription would miss.

`Next.js 16` · `FastAPI` · `SSE` · `Supabase` · `Gemini Vision` · `yt-dlp` · `ffmpeg`
